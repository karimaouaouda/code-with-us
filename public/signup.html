Frequently asked questions

Do I need any coding experience?

No. Every track starts from zero. You just need to be comfortable with a computer and ready to commit — your mentor adapts to your pace.

How much time do I need each week?

Plan for 8–10 hours a week on the full-time track, or around half that part-time. Live sessions are recorded if you miss one.

How much does it cost?

One price covers the full course, including mentorship and career support. See the pricing section on our Courses page.

Will you help me get a job?

Yes — CV reviews, mock interviews, and introductions to our hiring partners. Our career support continues until you sign your first developer role.

Questions first?

Talk to us before you apply. Tell us your background and we'll point you to the right track.
